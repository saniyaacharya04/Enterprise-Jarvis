Jarvis is an enterprise AI assistant designed to help employees quickly and accurately retrieve internal company knowledge through a conversational interface. It serves as a centralized access point for organizational information, reducing the time spent searching across documents, portals, and internal systems.

The assistant is built using a self-hosted large language model combined with a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architecture. Before generating any response, Jarvis retrieves relevant content from an internal knowledge base stored in a vector database. This ensures that all answers are grounded strictly in approved and curated documentation, eliminating hallucinations and maintaining high levels of accuracy and trust.

Employees can use Jarvis to ask natural language questions related to company policies, compliance requirements, internal processes, operational guidelines, and other organizational knowledge. The system is optimized to return precise, context-aware responses that reflect the most relevant internal information available.

Security and data privacy are core design principles of Jarvis. All data is stored securely within the organization’s infrastructure, and no sensitive information is sent to external services. The model, vector database, and retrieval pipeline are designed to operate entirely within controlled environments, making the solution suitable for enterprise and regulated settings.

Jarvis is model-agnostic by design, allowing organizations to integrate enterprise-approved language models while maintaining consistent retrieval and response behavior. This flexibility ensures the assistant can evolve alongside organizational requirements without compromising security, performance, or governance.
